🧠 **What is an Artifact**?

An artifact is something Claude builds for you that stands on its own: a document, a slide deck, a design, a dashboard, a prototype, or working code.

Instead of a long wall of text or code stuck inside the chat, you get the real thing in a separate window next to the conversation, ready to use, edit, and share.

💡 **Analogy**: The chat is the workshop where you talk and plan. The artifact is the finished product sitting on the table.

When does Claude make one? Claude makes an artifact when you ask for something you'll want to **edit, reuse, or share** (not just read once).

Want to be sure? Just say:

text
Create this as an artifact.

📦 Where do artifacts live?
Plan	                                               What happens
💎 **Paid plans** (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise)	Saved in the Artifacts tab. Come back anytime, keep editing, and share
🆓 **Free plan**	The artifact stays inside the chat that created it

Remember: **Conversation** = where you create. **Artifacts tab** = where your work lives.

🧩 Designs, Decks and Docs

Claude has three special tools for the most common work deliverables.

Tool	                             What it makes	                                                                              Cool features
🎨 **Claude Design**	        Landing pages, one-pagers, product mockups, interface prototypes	                                 Bring your design system so drafts match your brand. Drag, resize and align things on the canvas
📊 **Claude Slides**	        Presentations	Give it notes or a report and it outlines, writes and lays out every slide.          Edit any slide, add your images, present inside Claude
📄 **Claude Docs**	          "Living" documents you write together with Claude	                                                   Claude drafts live, asks questions, explains choices in comments. Several people can edit at once. Changes save automatically

They work together. You can say:

"Turn this doc into a deck."
"Make a one-pager from this deck."
📈 Charts in Docs

Claude can pull data from your connected tools into charts and diagrams inside a doc. ⚠️ It's a snapshot, not live. Ask Claude to refresh it when you want new numbers.

⚙️ Availability (beta)

Can't see them? Ask your admin.

🚀 Create your first artifact

**Two ways to start**:

In any chat, describe what you want. The composer's Output menu lets you pick design, slides or doc.
Open the Artifacts tab, pick a design, deck or doc, and describe your need.

Claude automatically uses what it already knows: your uploaded files, the conversation so far, project knowledge, and connected tools. You don't have to repeat yourself.

✍️ Example prompts
**Prompt**	                                                                                                  **What you get**
"Turn these meeting notes into a five-slide deck for my team meeting"                                  	An editable, presentable deck
"Draft a one-page project brief for the onboarding revamp as a doc I can share with the team"	           A shareable living doc
"Design a landing page for a productivity app with a hero section and feature list"	                      A design on an editable canvas
"Build an interactive dashboard that lets me input monthly expenses and see a breakdown"	                An interactive dashboard (works on any plan)

When it appears, you can copy or download it and keep refining.

✏️ **Three ways to edit an artifact**

You can mix all three freely.

Method	                      How it works
🖊️ **Edit directly**	       Type in a doc, edit a slide, or drag and resize on a design canvas. Your edits and Claude's sit side by side
💬 **Comment**	             Leave a comment on the exact slide or passage. Claude changes it there. In docs, @-mention Claude and it replies with what it changed and why
🗣️ **Just ask**             	Keep chatting and Claude makes another pass, showing every change

💡 For a tiny fix (typo, nudging a logo), editing it yourself is faster than describing it.

📱 Mobile note: Editing directly, commenting and changing sharing happen on desktop or web. On mobile you can ask for an artifact in a chat and open it full screen in the Artifacts tab.

🔗 Sharing your work

**Artifacts** are private until you share them. Anyone you share with needs a Claude account.

**Options for designs, decks and docs**:

Add people by name or email
Choose their permission: View, Comment, or Edit (docs offer View and Edit only)
Or share a link with everyone in your organization

What each plan allows:

Plan	                     **Sharing rules**
**Pro / Max**	              Keep private or share with anyone who has the link. You can publish a design or deck publicly. Only the artifact is public, your chat stays private
**Team / Enterprise**     	Links stay inside your organization by default. Sharing outside needs an Owner/Primary Owner to turn on External sharing in Organization settings > Artifacts
**Free**	                  Copy, download, or publish so anyone with the link can view
📤 Exporting

**When work needs to leave Claude**:

Artifact	             Export formats
📊 **Deck**           	.pptx, .pdf
🎨 **Design**	           .pptx, .pdf, .html
📄 **Doc**	             Google Docs, .docx

You can also copy or download directly from the artifact window.

🏆 Tips for better artifacts
Ask for the deliverable, not just the content.
❌ "Summarize our Q3 results" (you get a chat reply)
✅ "Turn our Q3 results into a one-page doc for the leadership team" (you get an artifact)
Be specific.
❌ "Build a budget tracker"
✅ "Build a monthly budget tracker where I can enter expenses by category, see a pie chart, and get a warning when I'm over budget"
Describe who will use it. A flowchart for new employees looks different from one for engineers.
Build on earlier work. The best artifacts come at the end of a conversation, once Claude understands the problem.
Refresh data when needed. Charts in docs don't update themselves. Ask Claude to refresh.

📖 **Glossary**
Term	                                                 Meaning
**Artifact**	                            A standalone output (doc, deck, design, dashboard, code) shown beside the chat
**Artifacts tab**	                        Where your saved artifacts live (paid plans)
**Claude Design / Slides / Docs**            	Dedicated tools for visuals, presentations and living documents (beta, paid)
**Canvas**	                                  The editable surface where you drag and resize design elements
**Design system**                            	Your brand's colors, fonts and styles, so designs match your brand
**Beta**                                     	A feature still being tested and improved
**Export**	                                  Saving an artifact as a file to use outside Claude
