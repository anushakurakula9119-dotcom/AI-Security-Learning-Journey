1. **Recap of the whole course**

Getting started with Claude (Lessons 1 to 2)
Claude is an AI assistant designed to be helpful, harmless, and honest. It's a thinking partner for complex work, not just a chatbot.
Available on web, desktop, and mobile, with conversations syncing across devices.
Effective prompts give context, define the task, and specify rules for format and style. (My notes: the 3-part checklist, stage / task / rules.)
Getting better results (Lesson 3)
Iteration matters. Treat first responses as starting points and refine through conversation.
Generic answers or the wrong tone can usually be fixed with more specific context.
**AI Fluency has four competencies**: Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence.
Organizing your work (Lessons 4 to 7)
Idea	In simple words
Projects	Workspaces with persistent knowledge, custom instructions, and team collaboration
Artifacts	Standalone outputs made alongside a conversation: documents, code, diagrams, interactive tools
Skills	Instruction packages that teach Claude specialized workflows: built-in document creation, plus custom skills I can create
Expanding Claude's reach (Lessons 8 to 10)
Idea	In simple words
Connectors	Link Claude to tools like Google Workspace, Slack, and Notion, so it works with my actual data
Enterprise Search	A dedicated project for searching across an organization's knowledge sources
Research	Systematic, multi-source investigations that would take hours or days by hand
Putting it all together (Lessons 11 to 12)
Claude applies across roles: sales, marketing, finance, HR, legal, research.
Beyond claude.ai: Claude Code, Slack, Excel, and Chrome.

3. **Closing message** (key takeaways)
The most important step is to start.
Skills improve with practice, and my sense of when Claude helps grows over time.
Start small: pick one recurring task this week (such as drafting an email, summarizing meeting notes, or analyzing a spreadsheet), then iterate.
Claude is a collaborator, not a replacement. My expertise, context, and judgment give the best results.
